# Task: Sessions last 400 days and renew daily

## Description

A device that keeps using the app never signs out. Sessions last 400 days,
the most a browser keeps a cookie, and renew at most once a day while in
use. This replaces accounts' 90-day lifetime (stakeholder, 2026-11-11). The
cookie cache stays off, so removing a member still signs them out on their
next request.

See the epic's "Sessions last 400 days and renew daily" and PRD user story 2.
Read the installed `better-auth` session options rather than memory.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `SESSION_MAX_AGE_SECONDS` is 400 days (`400 * 24 * 60 * 60`) and is
      Better Auth's `session.expiresIn`; `updateAge` stays one day. The
      constant's comment says why 400 (the browser cookie cap).
- [ ] The session cookie's `Max-Age` is 400 days, both on sign-in and on
      renewal.
- [ ] The cookie cache stays off.
- [ ] `scripts/check-auth.ts` asserts the new lifetime (its 90-day
      assertions and header comment are updated), that a session used after
      more than a day is renewed to 400 days from that use, that one used
      within the day is not renewed, and that a removed member is still
      refused on their next request.
- [ ] No "90 days" remains in `src` or `scripts`.
