# Family Meals

The app that takes weekly dinner planning off one household's plate: it drafts the week, the household approves it, and the groceries follow.

## Language

### People and access

**Family**:
The one household the app serves. There is exactly one; more than one is not supported.
_Avoid_: Household, team, group

**Member**:
A person in the Family who can sign in. The Owner is also a Member.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Owner**:
The one Member who invites and removes other Members.
_Avoid_: Admin

**Invitation**:
The Owner's permission for one email address to join the Family as a Member; it is pending, accepted or cancelled.
_Avoid_: Invite list, allowlist

**Session**:
One signed-in device belonging to a Member.
_Avoid_: Login
